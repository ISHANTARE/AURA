# AURA — Vision

> **AURA** stands for **AI-Unified Reality Assistant**
> *Your AI life operating system. One tap. You speak. Life organizes itself.*

---

## The Problem

Modern life fragments attention across dozens of apps.

You receive a deadline in Gmail.
You see a meeting in WhatsApp.
You read a submission notice in your college portal.
You mentally note a project task.

And then you forget. Or you scramble. Or you miss it.

The existing solutions all require YOU to do the organizing work:

- Open Google Calendar. Manually type the event.
- Open Todoist. Manually add the task.
- Set 3 separate reminders in your Clock app.
- Repeat for every new deadline.

No existing app solves this in a single, seamless, voice-first flow.

---

## The Vision

**AURA is a single-tap AI life assistant that listens, understands, and organizes.**

You see a notification about a deadline.
You press one button.
You say: *"ML assignment due August 3rd, midnight. Remind me 3 days before, 1 day before, and 2 hours before. Add 'Complete EDA' for tomorrow."*

AURA:

- Creates the task with all metadata
- Sets the deadline
- Creates 3 layered reminders automatically
- Adds the daily task
- Places everything in the right workspace
- Shows you a morning briefing the next day

**This is what having a personal assistant feels like.**

---

## Why Build It?

1. **No existing app does this end-to-end** — they all require manual data entry
2. **AI is finally good enough** to understand natural language intent reliably
3. **Privacy is non-negotiable** — most productivity apps surveil your data
4. **Students and professionals** lose enormous productivity to context-switching between apps
5. **This is a product worth building** — not just a tool to use

---

## What AURA Is

- An **AI-native productivity OS** running on top of Android (and eventually desktop)
- A **unified life timeline** — every task, event, deadline, and reminder in one place
- A **voice-first interface** — capture anything in seconds, hands-free
- A **workspace-aware system** — separates VIT life, IIT prep, internship, personal tasks
- A **privacy-first platform** — data lives on device by default

---

## What AURA Is NOT

- Not a calendar replacement that syncs with Google (optional, not required)
- Not a surveillance tool that reads WhatsApp or Gmail without permission
- Not just another to-do app with an AI coat of paint
- Not cloud-dependent for core features
- Not a minimal viable product — it should feel premium from day one

---

## The Central Philosophy

> **AURA owns the data model. Everything else is a mirror.**

Google Calendar, Outlook, Notion — these become optional sync layers.
AURA is the source of truth for your life.

Every important thing flows into AURA because **you choose to send it there**.

---

## Target User (v1)

**Ishan T** — BTech CSE student (VIT + IIT prep + internship + personal projects + health).

A person who:

- Has multiple parallel responsibilities across contexts
- Is highly intelligent but loses things in the chaos of modern app fragmentation
- Values privacy and owning their own data
- Wants to use AI tools, not be used by them
- Has the curiosity and will to build rather than just consume

---

## Long-Term Ambition

> One day, AURA becomes a product that any ambitious person — student, founder, researcher, professional — uses as their primary life interface.

The defining claim: *"Your data belongs to you. AURA works offline, stores everything locally by default, and only syncs externally when you explicitly enable it."*

This is rarer than it sounds. This is worth building.
