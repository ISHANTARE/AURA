Pixel-accurate visual guides for all core views, overlays, bottom sheets, and theme variations. Clean Bento layout with 2-part stats and reactive theme accents.

Restored clean Bento layout with split stats (Today's Tasks vs Overdue Alert), Urgent deadlines cell (60%), Glowing Voice Orb cell (40%), and Today's Focus task list.

Voice Capture & AI Intent Modal

Floating voice capture overlay with pulsing multi-layer glowing orb, real-time speech transcription bubble, and structured AI intent preview with confirm CTA.

  • • Transcription: Live STT feedback with pulsing listening cursor.
  • • AI Intent Box: Extracted title, workspace, deadline, and confidence badge.

Task Detail & Manual Edit Sheet

Modal bottom sheet with rounded corners (28px), workspace pill selector, date & time chips, 4-tier priority picker, interactive subtask checklist, and notes.

  • • Workspace Pills: Quick single-tap workspace assignment.
  • • Schedule Row: Independent date and time picker chips.
  • • Subtasks: Reorderable checklist with strike-through and progress count.
  • • Actions: Delete button (destructive) and Glowing Save CTA.
